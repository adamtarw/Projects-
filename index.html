What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms. Cells contain different organelles, and each organelle has a special job that helps the cell stay alive and function properly.

🌿 Plant Cell

A plant cell has a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large central vacuole.

🐾 Animal Cell

An animal cell has a nucleus, mitochondria and many other organelles.

🧬 Human Cell

Human cells are animal cells that make up the tissues and organs of the human body.

🌿 PLANT CELL

A plant cell is a eukaryotic cell found in plants. It contains special structures that help plants make their own food.

Plant Cell Organelles

Cell Wall

Provides protection, support and shape to the plant cell. It is mainly made of cellulose.

وظيفته: الحماية والدعم.

Cell Membrane

Controls what enters and leaves the cell.

وظيفته: تنظيم دخول وخروج المواد.

Nucleus

Contains DNA and controls the activities of the cell.

وظيفتها: التحكم في أنشطة الخلية.

Chloroplast

Contains chlorophyll and absorbs light energy for photosynthesis.

وظيفتها: البناء الضوئي.

Central Vacuole

Stores water and other substances and helps maintain cell pressure.

وظيفتها: تخزين الماء والمواد.

Mitochondria

Produces most of the energy used by the cell through cellular respiration.

وظيفتها: إنتاج الطاقة.

Ribosomes

Make proteins needed by the cell.

وظيفتها: تصنيع البروتين.

Golgi Apparatus

Modifies, sorts and packages proteins and other materials.

وظيفته: تعديل وتغليف المواد.

Endoplasmic Reticulum

Helps produce and transport proteins and lipids.

وظيفتها: التصنيع والنقل.

Cytoplasm

A jelly-like material where organelles are located and many reactions occur.

وظيفته: وسط الخلية.

🐾 ANIMAL CELL

An animal cell is a eukaryotic cell found in animals. It does not have a cell wall or chloroplasts.

Animal Cell Organelles

Nucleus

Controls cell activities and contains genetic information (DNA).

وظيفتها: التحكم في الخلية.

Nucleolus

Produces components needed to make ribosomes.

وظيفتها: تكوين أجزاء الريبوسومات.

Mitochondria

Produces energy through cellular respiration.

وظيفتها: إنتاج الطاقة.

Ribosomes

Responsible for protein synthesis.

وظيفتها: تصنيع البروتين.

Rough ER

Has ribosomes and helps make and transport proteins.

وظيفتها: تصنيع البروتين.

Smooth ER

Helps produce lipids and process certain substances.

وظيفتها: تصنيع الدهون.

Golgi Apparatus

Packages and distributes proteins and other materials.

وظيفته: التغليف والتوزيع.

Lysosomes

Contain enzymes that break down waste and damaged cell parts.

وظيفتها: الهضم الخلوي.

Centrioles

Help organize structures involved in cell division.

وظيفتها: المساعدة في انقسام الخلية.

Vacuoles

Store water, nutrients and waste materials.

وظيفتها: التخزين.

Cytoskeleton

Maintains cell shape and helps with movement and transport.

وظيفته: الدعم والحركة.

Cell Membrane

Controls the movement of substances into and out of the cell.

وظيفته: تنظيم المرور.

🧬 HUMAN CELL

Human cells are the basic building blocks of the human body. There are many types of human cells, and each type has a special function. Human cells are animal cells.

Types Of Human Cells

🧠 Nerve Cells

Transmit electrical and chemical signals throughout the body.

الخلايا العصبية: تنقل الإشارات.

❤️ Muscle Cells

Contract and relax to produce movement.

الخلايا العضلية: تساعد على الحركة.

🩸 Red Blood Cells

Carry oxygen from the lungs to the body's tissues.

خلايا الدم الحمراء: تنقل الأكسجين.

🛡️ White Blood Cells

Help protect the body against infections.

خلايا الدم البيضاء: الدفاع عن الجسم.

?? Bone Cells

Build, maintain and repair bone tissue.

الخلايا العظمية: بناء العظام.

🧴 Skin Cells

Form a protective barrier around the body.

خلايا الجلد: حماية الجسم.

Important Note

Human cells share many organelles with other animal cells, such as the nucleus, mitochondria, ribosomes and cell membrane. However, different human cell types have different shapes and functions.
